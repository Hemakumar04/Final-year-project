# Final-year-project
India being an agricultural country, its economy predominantly depends on agricultural yield and allied agro-industry products, but farming is highly affected by unpredictable rainfall and varying soil conditions such as Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium levels, soil moisture, surface temperature, and weather parameters like temperature and rainfall. To improve productivity through technology, the proposed Smart Irrigation System with Crop Recommendation and Animal Detection integrates real-time soil and weather monitoring with intelligent decision-making. Weather forecast data obtained from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) along with soil parameter datasets are analyzed using Machine Learning algorithms, specifically Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), to recommend the most suitable and profitable crop based on current environmental conditions through an Android application. The system also automates irrigation by activating water supply based on soil moisture levels to ensure efficient water usage. In addition, an ultrasonic sensor is incorporated for animal detection, where it continuously monitors field boundaries and detects intrusions by measuring distance variations; upon detection, alerts are generated through a buzzer and mobile notification to prevent crop damage. Thus, the integrated system enhances crop productivity, optimizes water management, protects fields from animal intrusion, and supports farmers in making data-driven agricultural decisions.
